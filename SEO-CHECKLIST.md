## 2. Immediate Post-Launch Steps for Shop Owner

### Step 1: Google Search Console Verification
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Choose **Domain** property and enter `royaldrivemechanics.ca`.
3. Copy the `google-site-verification=...` TXT record provided by Google.
4. Add the TXT record in your **Cloudflare DNS** dashboard.
5. Click **Verify** in Google Search Console.
6. Submit your sitemap:
   - Navigate to **Sitemaps** on the left menu.
   - Enter `sitemap-index.xml` (automatically generated by Astro).
   - Click **Submit**.

### Step 2: Google Business Profile (GBP) Configuration
1. Log into your Google Business Profile manager.
2. Ensure the primary category is set to **Auto Repair Shop** and secondary categories include:
   - *Mechanic*
   - *Brake Shop*
   - *Oil Change Service*
   - *Car Inspection Station*
   - *Auto Body Shop*
3. Update the **Website URL** to:
   `https://royaldrivemechanics.ca/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
4. Add the **Appointment Link**:
   `https://royaldrivemechanics.ca/book?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp_book`
5. Add all 7 services under the "Services" tab matching the website slugs.
6. Publish the **15% Off Brakes & Oil Changes** promotion under the "Offers" tab.
7. Upload high-resolution photos of the workshop exterior, bays, and vehicle repairs matching `/public/images/services/`.
