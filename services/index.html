Collision Work / Collision Repairs

We work directly with all major insurance providers.

From minor bumper scrapes, door dings, and fender repairs to major structural collision damage, Royal Drive Mechanics works directly with all major Canadian insurance companies to get you back on the road safely and stress-free.

How A Collision Repair Works:

Step 01
Assessment & Damage Documentation

We carefully inspect your vehicle's exterior panels, structural frame, and mechanical components, taking detailed photographic documentation of all damage.

Step 02
Insurance Estimate & Direct Coordination

We prepare an itemized repair estimate and submit it directly to your insurance company adjuster to secure quick authorization without delay.

Step 03
Precision Body & Mechanical Repair

Our certified technicians perform structural alignment, dent repairs, panel replacements, computer paint matching, and mechanical recalibration.

Step 04
Multi-Point Quality & Safety Check

Before handing back your keys, we perform a comprehensive quality audit, road test, and final vehicle wash to ensure your vehicle is returned in pristine condition.

What's Included in This Service:

  • ✓ Direct coordination and electronic estimating with all major Canadian insurance providers
  • ✓ Unibody and frame alignment assessment
  • ✓ Bumper, fender, quarter panel, and door repair or replacement
  • ✓ Computerized color-matched paint refinishing and clear coat blending
  • ✓ Post-collision mechanical inspection (suspension, steering, alignment, radiators)
  • ✓ ADAS sensor calibration and safety systems reset
